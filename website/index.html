Sent

Tincan is a Linux desktop app that reads and sends text messages through your iPhone. Leave the phone in your pocket: new texts show up on your desktop, where you can read them, copy sign-in codes, and reply right from your keyboard.

It connects like your car does

Over Bluetooth, the same way a car stereo reads your texts. No Mac, no account, no server or telemetry, and nothing to install on your iPhone.

Your iPhone still does the sending

Replies go out through your iPhone as normal texts. Everything Tincan keeps stays on your computer, saved encrypted.

A relay, not a replacement

Tincan handles new texts and quick replies while your iPhone stays the source of truth for your full history and media.

Sign-in codes, one click away

When a text contains a one-time code, Tincan highlights it and adds a Copy code button under the message and on its desktop notification.

Expect a little static.

Apple limits what an iPhone can share over Bluetooth. Tincan isn't as complete as Messages on a Mac. Here's what to expect.

Supported

  • Incoming iMessage and SMS
  • Replies and new 1:1 conversations
  • Read and unread status, synced with your iPhone
  • Desktop notifications that open the conversation
  • Contact names and photos
  • Search across your saved messages
  • Send status, with retry if a message fails
  • Works with your iPhone locked and the window closedWhen the background service is enabled.

Not supported (by Apple)

  • Photos, videos, voice notes, and filesYou'll see a note that an attachment arrived instead.
  • Messages sent from your iPhone itselfYou will only see the messages you send from Tincan.
  • Your full message historyiOS shares only the 10 most recent inbox messages on first sync. Tincan keeps a local history from then on.
  • Typing indicators and delivery or read receipts
  • Threaded replies, edits, unsend, and effects
  • Blue and green bubblesiMessage and SMS look the same over Bluetooth.
  • Sending to group chats

Agents welcome, by invitation only.

Let the AI agent you already use catch you up on what you missed, dig up that address from last month, or draft a reply you only have to approve. Everything it writes waits for you to send, and agent access is off until you turn it on.

You decide what agents can do

  • Off until you chooseIt's your call. Agents can't touch your messages until you decide how much to let them do.
  • Sensitive information stays privateAgents never see phone numbers, emails, or verification codes unless you deliberately allow it, and conversations you hide from agents stay out of their reach.
  • Review before it sendsWith "Ask me" mode, every message an agent writes waits in your composer as a draft for you to send, edit, or discard.
  • Time to change your mindBy default, an agent's message waits 10 seconds on your computer before it goes to your iPhone, so you can undo it.
  • Or take the guardrails offIf you trust your agent, "Full access" mode lets it read every conversation and send without asking, with no limitations. Every agent setting is yours to freely configure.
